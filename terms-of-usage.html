Platform Terms & Metered Usage Disclosure

This disclosure explains how Katalyst CRM bills subscription access versus metered, pay-as-you-go utilities. Metered usage charges are final and non-refundable once consumed.

1. Subscription Fee vs. Metered Utility Charges

Your fixed monthly software subscription covers workspace access, user seats, built-in features, and core platform infrastructure.

Certain features operating within the platform function as metered pay-as-you-go utilities. These metered services are billed dynamically based on exact account consumption and include:

  • Email Verification & List Validation
  • Outbound & Inbound Email Delivery
  • SMS Messaging & Phone Calls
  • Phone Number Hosting & Carrier Registrations
  • AI Content, Conversation, & Voice Processing
  • Premium Workflow & API Executions

2. Immediate Resource Consumption

Metered actions consume real-time external computing power, telecommunications infrastructure, and global database registry checks the instant they are triggered.

When an account user runs a bulk email verification, executes an email blast, or sends SMS sequences:

  • Digital requests are immediately routed to third-party verification networks and carrier gateways.
  • Server resources, processing power, and bandwidth are irrevocably expended at that exact timestamp.
  • The digital utility is considered 100% delivered at the moment the process is executed by the user or by automated workflows configured within the account.

3. Absolute Non-Refundability of Usage Charges

Because metered actions consume unrecoverable third-party server assets instantaneously, all usage-based charges, wallet reloads, and metered fees are final and strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.

Refund requests will not be granted for usage charges resulting from:

  • Uploading invalid, old, or poor-quality contact lists.
  • Bounced emails, failed message deliveries, or recipient server rejections.
  • Accidental triggers or incorrect workflow configurations set up by account users.
  • Account cancellations requested after metered actions have already executed.

4. Account Billing Responsibilities & Auto-Recharges

To ensure uninterrupted execution of system automations, metered features draw from your account wallet balance or charge your payment method on file based on predefined auto-recharge settings.

Account owners are solely responsible for monitoring their workflow settings, automated list-validation triggers, and wallet balance thresholds. Managing automated verification triggers or pausing outbound sequences remains the sole responsibility of the account holder.

5. Dispute & Chargeback Policy

Initiating a payment dispute or chargeback with a credit card issuer or payment processor for rendered metered usage, completed email verifications, or valid system consumption constitutes a breach of these Terms.

In the event of an unjustified chargeback attempt for delivered system resources:

  • Account access will be suspended immediately to prevent further utility consumption.
  • Detailed server usage logs, IP activity records, and time-stamped execution receipts will be submitted directly to the payment processor to defeat the dispute.
  • The account holder remains liable for all unpaid usage balances and associated collection or dispute processing fees.
